# PurposePath

## Overview
PurposePath is a comprehensive self-discovery app that helps users identify their core values, strengths, and life purpose through interactive assessments, AI-powered analysis, and purpose alignment exercises. It bridges the gap between mental health apps and productivity tools by helping users understand their "why" before optimizing their "how".

## Problem Solved
Many people feel lost or unfulfilled but lack a structured way to discover their core values, strengths, and life purpose. Existing mental health apps focus on meditation or symptom management, while productivity apps optimize tasks - neither helps users understand who they are at a fundamental level. PurposePath provides a guided journey of self-discovery that empowers users to make more meaningful life decisions aligned with their authentic selves.

## Target Audience
- Young professionals (25-40) experiencing mid-life crisis or career dissatisfaction
- Recent graduates feeling uncertain about their future direction
- People going through major life transitions (relocation
- divorce
- retirement)
- Anyone seeking more meaning and fulfillment in their daily life

## Key Features
1. **Interactive Values Assessment**: Guided questions and scenarios help users identify their top 5-7 core life values through reflection exercises and real-world scenario analysis
2. **Strengths Discovery Engine**: AI-powered analysis of user inputs, journal entries, and feedback to identify personal strengths and talents using established frameworks (VIA Character Strengths, CliftonStrengths-inspired)
3. **Purpose Alignment Dashboard**: Visual mapping showing how daily activities align with identified values and strengths, with suggestions for making meaningful changes
4. **Journal & Reflection Tools**: Private space for users to document their journey, track insights, and revisit past reflections
5. **Action Planning System**: Helps users create actionable steps to live more aligned with their discovered values and purpose

## Monetization Strategy
- **Freemium Model**: Basic values assessment free; premium features ($4.99/month or $39.99/year) include AI strengths analysis, unlimited journaling, advanced alignment tracking, and personalized coaching content
- **Corporate Wellness Packages**: B2B licensing for companies offering employee development programs
- **Affiliate Partnerships**: Curated book recommendations, courses, and resources related to personal growth

## Tech Stack Suggestions
- **Frontend**: React Native or Flutter for cross-platform mobile app; React.js for web dashboard
- **Backend**: Node.js with Express or Python FastAPI
- **Database**: PostgreSQL for structured data; MongoDB for journal entries and unstructured content
- **AI/ML**: Python with transformers library for NLP analysis; integrate with established psychometric frameworks
- **Cloud**: AWS or Google Cloud Platform for hosting and scalability
