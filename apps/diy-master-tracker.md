# DIY Master Tracker

## Overview
DIY Master Tracker is a comprehensive home improvement project management app that helps homeowners and DIY enthusiasts track multiple ongoing projects, manage materials inventory, document progress with photos/videos, and collaborate with family members or contractors. It bridges the gap between simple checklists and complex professional construction software.

## Problem Solved
Homeowners and DIY enthusiasts struggle to:
- Track multiple ongoing renovation projects simultaneously
- Manage material inventories with supplier links and cost tracking
- Document project progress with visual milestones (photos/videos)
- Share project plans and updates with family members or contractors
- Coordinate collaborative household improvement work

Existing solutions are either too simple (basic checklists) or too complex (professional construction management software), leaving a gap for mid-level DIY projects.

## Target Audience
- Homeowners undertaking renovation projects (kitchens
- bathrooms
- flooring)
- DIY enthusiasts building furniture or home improvements
- Property managers coordinating maintenance work
- Families working together on household projects

## Key Features
1. **Project Timeline Visualization** - Gantt-style views showing dependencies between tasks and milestones**
2. **Smart Material List Generator** - Auto-calculates quantities based on project dimensions and includes supplier recommendations with cost tracking**
3. **Collaborative Workspace** - Multiple family members can contribute photos, notes, and updates to shared projects**
4. **Before/After Comparison Tools** - Visual timeline of project progress with photo documentation**
5. **Hardware Store Integration** - Real-time pricing from hardware store APIs for material cost estimation**

## Monetization Strategy
- **Freemium Model**: Basic project tracking free; premium features ($4.99/month) include advanced analytics, unlimited projects, and API integrations
- **Affiliate Revenue**: Commission from hardware store purchases through supplier links
- **Pro Version**: One-time purchase ($29.99) for lifetime access to all features
