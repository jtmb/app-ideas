# LegacyLock

## Overview
LegacyLock is a digital estate management platform that helps people preserve their digital memories and assets for future generations. It provides a secure vault for storing photos, documents, videos, and links, along with automated letter delivery to loved ones and smart unlocking systems that release content at specific times or events.

## Problem Solved
People accumulate valuable digital assets (photos, documents, social media accounts, crypto wallets) but have no organized way to preserve memories or distribute digital assets after passing away. Families often struggle to access and manage these important digital legacies during grief, leading to lost memories and inaccessible digital treasures.

## Target Audience
- Adults aged 30-65 who want to preserve family memories for future generations
- Parents wanting to leave a digital legacy for their children/grandchildren
- Anyone with valuable digital assets (photos
- documents
- crypto) they want to protect and distribute after death

## Key Features
1. **Digital Asset Vault**: Securely store photos, videos, documents, and links with encryption; organize by category (family memories, work projects, creative works)
2. **Automated Letter Delivery**: Write letters to family/friends that automatically send on specific dates or anniversaries, even after the user passes away
3. **Smart Unlocking System**: Set time-based access rules (e.g., "unlock photo album 1 year after death certificate uploaded") and beneficiary distribution of digital assets

## Monetization Strategy
- Freemium model: Basic vault storage free, premium features ($5-10/month) for advanced unlocking rules, larger storage, and priority support
- One-time legacy planning package ($99) for comprehensive estate setup with legal document integration
- Family plan discounts for multiple users managing related digital estates

## Tech Stack Suggestions
- **Frontend**: React Native (cross-platform mobile app), React.js (web dashboard)
- **Backend**: Node.js with Express or Python FastAPI
- **Database**: PostgreSQL (relational data), AWS S3 or Google Cloud Storage (encrypted file storage)
- **Other**: AES-256 encryption, JWT authentication, scheduled task workers for letter delivery
