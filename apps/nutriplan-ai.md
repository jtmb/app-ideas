# NutriPlan AI

## Overview
NutriPlan AI is a personalized nutrition and meal planning assistant that uses advanced machine learning to create customized dietary plans based on individual health goals, preferences, allergies, and lifestyle. It seamlessly integrates with grocery shopping, recipe generation, and progress tracking to help users achieve optimal nutrition effortlessly.

## Problem Solved
- Difficulty creating balanced, personalized meal plans
- Food waste from unused ingredients and recipes
- Lack of time to plan nutritious meals
- Struggles with dietary restrictions (allergies, preferences, medical conditions)
- No unified system for nutrition tracking, shopping, and recipe management
- Confusion about portion sizes and nutritional content

## Target Audience
- Health-conscious individuals seeking weight management or fitness goals
- People with dietary restrictions (vegan
- gluten-free
- keto
- diabetes-friendly)
- Busy professionals needing quick
- nutritious meal solutions
- Families looking to improve overall nutrition at home
- Post-surgery or medical patients requiring specific dietary guidance
- Athletes optimizing performance through nutrition

## Key Features
1. **AI-Powered Personalization**: Analyzes user's health data (age, weight, activity level, goals), dietary preferences, allergies, and medical conditions to create highly customized meal plans that adapt over time based on progress.
2. **Smart Grocery Integration**: Automatically generates shopping lists from weekly meal plans; integrates with major grocery delivery services (Instacart, Walmart+, Amazon Fresh) for one-click ordering; tracks pantry inventory to reduce food waste.
3. **Recipe Generation & Adaptation**: Creates original recipes based on available ingredients and dietary needs; adapts existing recipes for different calorie targets, allergen restrictions, or cooking skill levels; provides step-by-step cooking instructions with video tutorials.
4. **Nutrition Tracking & Analytics**: Real-time macro/micronutrient tracking with visual progress charts; AI insights identifying nutritional gaps and suggesting adjustments; integration with wearable devices (Fitbit, Apple Watch) for activity-based meal recommendations.
5. **Social Accountability & Community**: Share meal plans with friends/family for accountability; join challenge groups (e.g., "30-Day Healthy Eating Challenge"); access community recipes and success stories; get personalized feedback from nutritionists (premium feature).

## Monetization Strategy
- **Freemium Model**: Basic meal planning and tracking free; premium AI personalization, unlimited recipes, and advanced analytics at $9.99/month or $79/year
- **Affiliate Revenue**: Earn commissions from grocery delivery partnerships, supplement recommendations, and kitchen equipment sales
- **Corporate Wellness Programs**: B2B licensing for employee health benefits packages
- **Premium Nutritionist Consultations**: One-on-one sessions with certified nutritionists ($50-100/session)
- **Brand Partnerships**: Collaborate with healthy food brands for sponsored content and product placements

## Tech Stack Suggestions
- **Frontend**: React Native (cross-platform mobile app), Next.js (web dashboard)
- **Backend**: Python/Django or Node.js/Express for API services
- **Database**: PostgreSQL (user data, recipes), MongoDB (meal plans, shopping lists)
- **AI/ML**: TensorFlow/PyTorch for recommendation engines and personalization algorithms
- **APIs**: Nutritionix API (nutritional data), Spoonacular API (recipes), Stripe (payments)
- **Cloud**: AWS or Google Cloud Platform for hosting and machine learning infrastructure
- **Other**: WebSockets for real-time updates, Firebase for push notifications
