# ServiceMatch Local

## Overview
ServiceMatch Local is a trusted marketplace connecting homeowners and renters with verified local service professionals (plumbers, electricians, tutors, cleaners, etc.). The app provides transparent pricing ranges, credential verification, and AI-powered matching to simplify finding reliable service providers.

## Problem Solved
Finding and booking trusted local service providers is often frustrating due to:
- Lack of transparency in pricing
- Difficulty verifying credentials and qualifications
- No centralized platform for reviews and comparisons
- Time-consuming search process across multiple platforms

## Target Audience
- **Primary**: Homeowners
- renters
- and small business owners seeking reliable local services
- **Secondary**: Independent contractors and tradespeople looking to expand their customer base

## Key Features
1. **Verified Professional Profiles**: Service providers must submit licenses, insurance, certifications, and customer reviews for verification before appearing in the marketplace
2. **AI-Powered Matching & Instant Estimates**: Users describe their service need (e.g., "leaky faucet repair") and get matched with qualified local professionals who provide instant price range estimates
3. **Booking & Payment Integration**: Built-in scheduling system with secure payment processing, service completion tracking, and automated review requests
4. **Transparent Pricing Ranges**: See estimated costs before booking to avoid surprise charges
5. **Service Completion Tracking**: Monitor job progress from booking to completion with photo updates and status notifications

## Monetization Strategy
- **Commission Model**: 10-15% fee on completed service bookings
- **Featured Listings**: Service providers pay for premium visibility in search results
- **Subscription Plans**: Monthly/annual subscriptions for professionals (unlimited leads, advanced analytics, priority support)
- **Lead Generation Fees**: Charge for qualified lead referrals

## Tech Stack Suggestions
- **Frontend**: React Native or Flutter for cross-platform mobile app; Next.js for web portal
- **Backend**: Node.js with Express or Python FastAPI
- **Database**: PostgreSQL for relational data; Redis for caching and real-time features
- **AI/ML**: Python with TensorFlow/PyTorch for matching algorithms; NLP for service request parsing
- **Cloud**: AWS or Google Cloud Platform
- **Payment Integration**: Stripe Connect for marketplace payments
- **Maps & Location**: Google Maps API or Mapbox
