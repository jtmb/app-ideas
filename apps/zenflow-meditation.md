# ZenFlow Meditation

## Overview
ZenFlow is a structured, progressive meditation platform that combines science-backed breathing exercises with guided meditation and sleep stories. It adapts to user experience levels and integrates with wearables for comprehensive stress tracking.

## Problem Solved
Provides a structured, progressive meditation platform with guided breathing exercises and sleep stories to help users reduce stress, improve focus, and achieve better sleep through intentional mindfulness practice. Unlike general wellness apps, ZenFlow focuses specifically on the science-backed benefits of breathwork and meditation with adaptive difficulty levels.

## Target Audience
- Professionals experiencing burnout
- Students needing focus improvement
- Individuals seeking stress relief and better sleep quality

## Key Features
1. **Guided Breathing Exercises**: Box breathing, 4-7-8 technique, alternate nostril breathing with real-time visual feedback
2. **Progressive Meditation Library**: Organized by duration and difficulty level that adapts to user experience
3. **Sleep Stories & Ambient Soundscapes**: Relaxing narratives and sounds for better sleep quality
4. **Wearable Integration**: Syncs with fitness trackers for stress tracking and mindfulness insights
5. **Community Challenges**: Accountability features through group meditation challenges

## Monetization Strategy
- Freemium model: Basic breathing exercises free, premium content subscription ($4.99/month)
