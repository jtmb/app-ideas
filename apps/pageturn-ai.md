# PageTurn AI

## Overview
PageTurn AI is an intelligent reading companion that uses AI to match books to your current mood and provides chapter-by-chapter previews with personalized "skip advice" to help you decide whether to continue reading. It solves the problem of book discovery fatigue by offering curated recommendations based on emotional state rather than just genre tags.

## Problem Solved
People struggle to discover books that match their current mood, reading level, and interests. Traditional recommendation systems rely on genre tags and past reading history, which can create echo chambers and miss serendipitous discoveries. Additionally, many readers want to know "what's in it for me" before committing to a book but find blurbs too vague.

## Target Audience
- Adult readers (25-55) who:
- Feel overwhelmed by the 100
- + books available and want curated recommendations
- Use audiobooks but want to read physical/digital books too
- Seek personal growth through reading but struggle with selection
- Experience "book fatigue" from generic algorithmic recommendations

## Key Features
1. **MoodSync Engine**: AI-powered mood detection via optional voice/text input that matches books to emotional states (e.g., "feeling anxious" → recommends calming memoirs or uplifting fiction)
2. **Chapter Preview Cards**: One-page summaries of each chapter with "skip if..." and "continue because..." advice based on user's stated goals
3. **Reading Streak Social**: Gamified reading challenges where users can join book clubs focused on specific moods or themes rather than just genres
4. **Vocabulary Builder**: Contextual word learning integrated into the reading experience
5. **Connection Mapping**: Links books within the same narrative arc and suggests related reads

## Monetization Strategy
- **Freemium Model**: Free basic recommendations; premium features ($4.99/month) for unlimited mood sync, advanced chapter previews, and social features
- **Affiliate Revenue**: Book purchase links to Amazon/Bookshop.org with commission
- **Corporate Licensing**: Team reading programs for companies focused on employee development
- **Premium Content**: Curated reading lists from authors and literary experts
