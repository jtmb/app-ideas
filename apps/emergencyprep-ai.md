# EmergencyPrep AI

## Overview
EmergencyPrep AI is a comprehensive emergency preparedness and safety management app that helps individuals and families create, maintain, and execute emergency plans. It combines AI-powered risk assessment with real-time alert systems and family coordination tools.

## Problem Solved
Most families lack a unified system for emergency preparedness, leading to confusion during crises. People don't know what supplies they need, where family members are, or how to respond to different types of emergencies (natural disasters, medical emergencies, security threats). Current solutions are fragmented across multiple apps and documents.

## Target Audience
- Families with children or elderly members who need coordinated emergency planning
- Homeowners in disaster-prone areas (flood zones
- earthquake regions
- wildfire areas)
- Renters and apartment dwellers who need portable emergency preparedness solutions
- Parents concerned about school safety and child welfare during emergencies
- Individuals seeking to improve their personal safety readiness

## Key Features
1. **AI Risk Assessment**: Analyzes user's location data, local hazards, demographics to generate personalized emergency preparedness checklist and recommendations
2. **Smart Supply Tracker**: Digital inventory of emergency supplies with barcode scanning, expiration date alerts, and automatic reorder suggestions based on household size
3. **Family Coordination Hub**: Real-time check-in system, digital meeting point mapping, automated group messaging during emergencies, and last-known-location tracking
4. **Emergency Action Plans**: AI-generated customized response plans for different scenarios (fire, earthquake, flood, medical emergency) with step-by-step instructions and local resource integration

## Monetization Strategy
- Freemium model: Basic features free, premium subscription ($4.99/month or $39.99/year) for advanced AI features and unlimited family members
- Affiliate partnerships with emergency supply retailers
- Local business partnerships (emergency services, insurance companies)
- Enterprise version for HOAs, schools, and community organizations

## Tech Stack Suggestions
- **Frontend**: React Native or Flutter for cross-platform mobile app
- **Backend**: Node.js with Express or Python FastAPI
- **Database**: PostgreSQL for relational data + Redis for real-time features
- **Maps & Location**: Google Maps API or Mapbox
- **Push Notifications**: Firebase Cloud Messaging
- **AI/ML**: Python-based risk assessment models, NLP for emergency plan generation
- **Cloud**: AWS or Google Cloud Platform
