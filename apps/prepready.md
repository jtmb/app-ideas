# PrepReady

## Overview
PrepReady is a comprehensive emergency preparedness app that helps households track essential supplies, create evacuation plans, and coordinate with family members during crises. It provides AI-powered inventory management, scenario-based checklists, and secure family communication features to ensure everyone is ready for any disaster.

## Problem Solved
Most households lack a centralized system for emergency preparedness, making it difficult to track essential supplies, create evacuation plans, and coordinate with family members during crises. Current solutions are either too generic or require manual updates that people neglect. PrepReady addresses this by providing an automated, user-friendly system that encourages regular maintenance of emergency readiness.

## Target Audience
- Homeowners and renters with families (especially those with children or elderly members)
- People living in disaster-prone areas
- Anyone seeking to improve household safety preparedness

## Key Features
1. **Smart Supply Tracker**: AI-powered inventory management that tracks expiration dates of food, water, medications, and first aid supplies with automatic reorder alerts
2. **Family Communication Hub**: Secure messaging system for family members to share real-time status during emergencies, plus digital evacuation plans and meeting point coordination
3. **Scenario-Based Checklists**: Customizable emergency response guides for fires, floods, earthquakes, power outages, and other disasters with step-by-step instructions

## Monetization Strategy
- Freemium model: Basic supply tracking free, premium features ($2.99/month or $29.99/year) include advanced analytics, unlimited family members, and offline capabilities
- One-time purchase option for lifetime access ($49.99)
- Optional partnerships with emergency supply retailers for affiliate commissions on reorder links
