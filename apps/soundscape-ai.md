# SoundScape AI

## Overview
SoundScape AI is a generative AI-powered ambient audio environment creator that dynamically generates and adapts personalized soundscapes in real-time based on user input, time of day, weather conditions, and biometric data. Unlike static meditation apps or music streaming services, it creates unique, never-repeating audio blends tailored to your current needs.

## Problem Solved
Users struggle to find the perfect ambient soundscape for focus, relaxation, or sleep. Existing solutions require manual selection of pre-recorded tracks that may not match their current mood, environment, or needs in real-time. SoundScape AI solves this by using generative AI to create and adapt ambient audio environments dynamically.

## Target Audience
- Remote workers
- students
- creatives
- insomniacs
- and anyone seeking personalized audio environments for focus
- relaxation
- or better sleep.

## Key Features
1. **AI-Generated Soundscapes**: Creates unique ambient audio blends (rain + forest birds + soft piano) that never repeat exactly
2. **Adaptive Environment**: Adjusts soundscape in real-time based on user preferences, time of day, weather conditions, or connected biometric data (stress levels, sleep quality)
3. **Focus Mode Integration**: Syncs with productivity apps to provide optimal sound environments for deep work, study, or creative sessions
4. **Voice Control**: Natural language commands to modify soundscape parameters on the fly
5. **Multi-Device Sync**: Seamlessly transitions between devices while maintaining your personalized audio environment

## Monetization Strategy
- **Freemium Model**: Free tier with basic soundscapes and limited customization; Premium subscription ($4.99/month) for unlimited AI generation, biometric integration, and advanced features
- **Corporate Licensing**: B2B packages for remote teams and educational institutions
- **Hardware Partnerships**: Pre-installed on smart speakers and sleep devices

## Tech Stack Suggestions
- **Frontend**: React Native (cross-platform mobile), React.js (web dashboard)
- **Backend**: Node.js with Express, Python/FastAPI for AI audio generation
- **Database**: PostgreSQL (user data), Redis (real-time session management)
- **Audio Processing**: Web Audio API, TensorFlow/PyTorch for generative audio models
- **Other**: WebSocket for real-time streaming, Weather APIs, HealthKit/Google Fit integration
