# StudySync AI

## Overview
StudySync AI is an intelligent study companion that combines smart scheduling, progress tracking, and AI-powered motivation to help students stay organized and productive during their academic journey. It optimizes study sessions based on personal energy levels and subject difficulty while providing real-time coaching through an AI Study Buddy.

## Problem Solved
Students struggle to stay organized and productive during study sessions, often losing track of progress, forgetting what they've studied, and lacking motivation. There is no dedicated app that combines study session tracking with AI-powered productivity coaching tailored specifically to academic goals.

## Target Audience
- High school students
- college/university students
- and lifelong learners who need help staying organized and motivated during their academic journey.

## Key Features
1. **Smart Study Planner**: AI-powered scheduling that optimizes study sessions based on subject difficulty, personal energy levels, and optimal learning times using spaced repetition algorithms
2. **Progress Dashboard**: Visual tracking of study hours, subjects covered, quiz scores, and goal completion with customizable targets for exams or courses
3. **AI Study Buddy**: Chat-based companion that provides motivation, answers quick questions, generates practice quizzes, and offers personalized productivity tips during study sessions
4. **Spaced Repetition System**: Automatically schedules review sessions based on the forgetting curve to maximize retention
5. **Collaborative Study Groups**: Connect with classmates for virtual study sessions and peer accountability

## Monetization Strategy
- **Freemium Model**: Basic features free, premium subscription ($4.99/month or $39.99/year) for advanced AI coaching and unlimited study groups
- **Institutional Licensing**: Schools and universities can purchase bulk licenses for student populations
- **Affiliate Partnerships**: Recommend educational resources, courses, and study materials with affiliate commissions

## Tech Stack Suggestions
- **Frontend**: React Native or Flutter for cross-platform mobile app, React.js for web dashboard
- **Backend**: Node.js with Express or Python FastAPI for AI integration
- **Database**: PostgreSQL for user data, Redis for caching session data
- **AI/ML**: Python with TensorFlow/PyTorch for scheduling algorithms, LLM API (OpenAI/Claude) for Study Buddy chat functionality
- **Other**: Firebase for real-time notifications, AWS S3 for document storage
