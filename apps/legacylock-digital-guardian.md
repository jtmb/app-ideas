# LegacyLock Digital Guardian

## Overview
LegacyLock is a comprehensive digital estate planning and memory preservation app that helps users organize, secure, and transfer their valuable digital assets to designated beneficiaries after they're gone. It provides encrypted storage with automated release mechanisms to ensure irreplaceable memories and important information are preserved for future generations.

## Problem Solved
People accumulate vast amounts of digital assets (photos, documents, accounts, memories) but have no organized system to preserve and transfer these valuable digital legacies to loved ones after they're gone. This leads to permanent loss of irreplaceable memories and important information. Unlike existing ideas that focus on general productivity, health, or smart home management, LegacyLock specifically addresses the critical but overlooked need for digital estate planning and long-term memory preservation with secure, time-locked digital legacies and automated release mechanisms.

## Target Audience
- Digital natives aged 30-65 who have accumulated significant digital assets
- parents wanting to preserve family memories for grandchildren
- professionals concerned about protecting sensitive work documents
- and anyone who values their digital footprint as part of their legacy.

## Key Features
1. **Secure Vault System**: Encrypted storage for photos, videos, documents, and personal messages with granular access controls and inheritance rules
2. **Automated Release Mechanisms**: Time-delayed or event-triggered release of content to designated beneficiaries (e.g., "release wedding album 5 years after creation" or "send all financial docs to children when I pass")
3. **Digital Asset Inventory & Organization**: AI-powered categorization and tagging of digital assets, plus integration with cloud services (Google Drive, iCloud, Dropbox) to create a complete inventory

## Monetization Strategy
- **Freemium Model**: Basic vault storage free, premium features ($4.99/month or $49.99/year) for advanced encryption, unlimited storage, and multiple beneficiary management
- **One-time Purchase Option**: Lifetime access at $199.99 for users who prefer upfront payment
- **Family Plan**: Up to 5 family members for $29.99/month with shared vault capabilities

## Tech Stack Suggestions
- **Frontend**: React Native (cross-platform mobile app) or Flutter
- **Backend**: Node.js with Express or Python with FastAPI
- **Database**: PostgreSQL for structured data, AWS S3 for encrypted file storage
- **Other**: AES-256 encryption, AI/ML for asset categorization, OAuth 2.0 for cloud service integrations
