# ParentPulse

## Overview
ParentPulse is a centralized family command center app designed to help parents coordinate schedules, share important information, and stay organized when managing multiple children's activities, school communications, and family logistics across different devices.

## Problem Solved
Parents struggle to coordinate schedules, share important information, and stay organized when managing multiple children's activities, school communications, and family logistics across different devices. Existing solutions don't specifically address the unique needs of parents coordinating multiple children's needs.

## Target Audience
- Parents of school-aged children (ages 5-18)
- particularly families with multiple children or busy schedules who need centralized organization tools.

## Key Features
1. **Shared Family Calendar**: Syncs school events, activities, appointments across all family members with color-coded categories for each child
2. **Quick Communication Hub**: In-app messaging for parents to coordinate, plus direct lines to teachers and pediatricians (with privacy controls)
3. **Document Vault**: Secure storage for immunization records, report cards, medical information, and emergency contacts

## Monetization Strategy
- Freemium model: Basic calendar and communication features free
- Premium subscription ($4.99/month or $49.99/year): Advanced document management, unlimited messaging history, priority support, family plan discounts
- B2B partnerships: School districts can license for bulk student/parent access

## Tech Stack Suggestions
- **Frontend**: React Native (cross-platform iOS/Android) or Flutter
- **Backend**: Node.js with Express or Python FastAPI
- **Database**: PostgreSQL (relational data for schedules, users) + Firebase Storage (documents)
- **Other**: 
- **Real-Time Updates**: Socket.io or Pusher
- **Cloud Storage**: AWS S3 or Google Cloud Storage
- **Authentication**: Auth0 or Firebase Auth
- **Calendar Integration**: Google Calendar API, Apple CalendarKit
