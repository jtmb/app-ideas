# SafeHaven Prep

## Overview
SafeHaven Prep is a comprehensive emergency preparedness app that helps individuals and families create personalized disaster readiness plans, track essential supplies, and coordinate family communication during emergencies. It addresses the critical gap in organized emergency planning by providing location-based risk assessments and smart inventory management.

## Problem Solved
Most people lack a comprehensive, organized system for emergency preparedness. They don't know what supplies to stockpile, how to communicate with family during disasters, or how to assess their specific risk profile based on location and household needs. Existing apps focus on fitness, mental health, finance, or general productivity - none address the critical gap in emergency preparedness.

## Target Audience
- Homeowners and renters with families
- People living in disaster-prone areas (flood zones
- earthquake regions
- wildfire areas)
- Outdoor enthusiasts and campers
- Anyone who wants to be better prepared for emergencies but doesn't know where to start

## Key Features
1. **Personalized Risk Assessment**: Analyzes user's location (urban/rural, flood zone, earthquake risk) and household composition to generate customized emergency plans
2. **Smart Supply Inventory**: Tracks essential supplies (food, water, medications, first aid) with expiration date alerts and shopping list generation
3. **Family Communication Hub**: Creates emergency contact lists, shares real-time status updates during disasters, and coordinates family reunification points

## Monetization Strategy
- **Freemium Model**: Basic risk assessment and supply tracking free; premium features ($4.99/month) include advanced analytics, offline maps, and priority support
- **One-time Purchase**: Complete emergency kit checklist bundle ($14.99 one-time)
- **Partnerships**: Affiliate commissions from emergency supply retailers and insurance companies

## Tech Stack Suggestions
- **Frontend**: React Native (cross-platform mobile app), Flutter (alternative)
- **Backend**: Node.js with Express, Firebase (for real-time features)
- **Database**: PostgreSQL (structured data), MongoDB (flexible supply tracking)
- **Maps & Location**: Google Maps API or Mapbox for location-based risk assessment
- **Other**: Twilio API for SMS notifications, Pusher/Socket.io for real-time family updates
