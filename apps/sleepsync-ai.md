# SleepSync AI

## Overview
SleepSync AI is a proactive sleep environment optimization app that integrates with smart home devices to automatically adjust your bedroom environment (lights, temperature, noise) based on real-time analysis of sleep patterns, weather forecasts, local conditions, and stress indicators. Unlike passive trackers or alarm apps, it actively creates the perfect sleep conditions before bedtime.

## Problem Solved
Millions struggle with sleep quality due to inconsistent sleep schedules, environmental factors (light, temperature, noise), and stress. Current solutions are either passive trackers that only monitor sleep or alarm apps that wake users up - but none proactively optimize the entire sleep environment in real-time based on individual patterns and external conditions.

## Target Audience
- Insomniacs and people with poor sleep quality (estimated 30% of adults)
- Tech-savvy individuals who use smart home devices
- Shift workers needing flexible but optimized sleep schedules
- Parents seeking better sleep for themselves and their families

## Key Features
1. **Smart Environment Automation**: Integrates with smart home devices (Philips Hue, Nest, Sonos) to automatically dim lights, adjust temperature, and play white noise 30 minutes before optimal bedtime
2. **AI-Powered Sleep Pattern Analysis**: Uses machine learning to identify personal sleep triggers and optimize wake-up times based on sleep cycles for maximum alertness
3. **Real-Time Environmental Monitoring**: Connects with weather APIs, local noise sensors, and air quality data to proactively adjust environment when conditions might disrupt sleep
4. **Personalized Sleep Schedule Optimization**: Learns your circadian rhythm and suggests optimal bedtime and wake-up times based on historical data
5. **Stress & Wellness Integration**: Pulls data from wearable devices (Apple Health, Google Fit) to correlate stress levels with sleep quality and provide actionable insights

## Monetization Strategy
- Freemium model: Basic tracking free, premium automation features at $9.99/month
- Smart device partnerships: Affiliate revenue from recommended devices
- Corporate wellness programs: B2B licensing for employee health benefits
- Premium analytics: Advanced pattern recognition and predictive insights at $14.99/month

## Tech Stack Suggestions
- **Frontend**: React Native (iOS/Android) with smart home SDKs
- **Backend**: Node.js with Express, Python microservices for ML models
- **Database**: PostgreSQL for user data, TimescaleDB for time-series sleep data
- **AI/ML**: TensorFlow.js for edge inference, scikit-learn for pattern recognition
- **Integrations**: Smart home APIs (Matter protocol), wearable device SDKs (Apple Health, Google Fit)
