# AsyncFlow Hub

## Overview
AsyncFlow Hub is a centralized collaboration platform designed for remote-first teams that combines smart documentation, async meeting integration, and decision tracking to eliminate communication fragmentation across multiple platforms.

## Problem Solved
Distributed teams struggle with fragmented communication across multiple platforms (Slack, email, Zoom) and lack a centralized space for async documentation, decision tracking, and context-rich collaboration that doesn't require real-time meetings.

## Target Audience
- Remote-first startups (10-50 employees)
- distributed tech teams
- and hybrid organizations struggling with communication fragmentation and meeting overload.

## Key Features
1. **Smart Documentation Hub**: Centralized knowledge base with AI-powered search that automatically links related decisions, meeting notes, and project updates across threads
2. **Async Meeting Integration**: Transforms video calls into searchable documentation with auto-generated summaries, action items, and context preservation for team members who can't attend live
3. **Decision Tracker**: Visual timeline of team decisions with voting, rationale storage, and automatic follow-up reminders to ensure accountability without micromanagement

## Monetization Strategy
- Freemium model: Free tier for up to 5 users with basic features
- Pro plan ($12/user/month): Advanced search, unlimited documentation, decision tracking
- Enterprise plan ($25/user/month): Custom integrations, SSO, advanced analytics, dedicated support
