# StyleSustain Tracker

## Overview
StyleSustain Tracker is a comprehensive app that helps environmentally conscious consumers track their clothing purchases, monitor garment longevity, and calculate their environmental impact. It combines personal wardrobe management with sustainability metrics to empower users to make more sustainable fashion choices.

## Problem Solved
Consumers want to make more sustainable fashion choices but lack tools to track their clothing purchases, monitor garment longevity, and calculate their environmental impact. There's no comprehensive solution that combines wardrobe management with sustainability metrics, leaving people unable to understand the true cost of their fashion consumption or identify areas for improvement.

## Target Audience
- Environmentally conscious consumers aged 18-45 who are interested in sustainable living
- minimalism
- and ethical fashion. Particularly appeals to millennials and Gen Z who want to reduce their environmental footprint while maintaining style.

## Key Features
1. **Wardrobe Carbon Calculator**: Tracks CO2 emissions from each clothing purchase based on fabric type, origin, and production methods; provides visual impact dashboard
2. **Garment Longevity Tracker**: Logs when items were bought, tracks wear frequency, sends repair/maintenance reminders to extend clothing life, calculates 'cost per wear'
3. **Sustainable Brand Directory**: Curated database of eco-friendly brands with transparency scores (materials, labor practices, certifications) and shopping recommendations
4. **Wardrobe Analytics**: Visual insights into spending patterns, carbon footprint trends, and sustainability score improvements over time
5. **Community Challenges**: Join challenges to reduce fast fashion consumption, share sustainable style tips, and compete on eco-friendly metrics

## Monetization Strategy
- **Freemium Model**: Basic tracking features free; premium subscription ($4.99/month or $39.99/year) for advanced analytics, unlimited items, and brand directory access
- **Affiliate Commissions**: Earn commissions from sustainable brand purchases made through the app's shopping recommendations
- **Brand Partnerships**: Partner with eco-friendly brands for featured listings and sponsored content (clearly marked to users)
