# SafeGuard Prep

## Overview
SafeGuard Prep is a comprehensive emergency preparedness app that helps individuals and families create personalized safety plans, track essential supplies, receive real-time alerts, and coordinate with local emergency services. It empowers users to be better prepared for disasters through organized planning and timely information.

## Problem Solved
Most people are unprepared for emergencies due to lack of organization and timely information. SafeGuard Prep addresses the critical gap in personal safety planning where families struggle to maintain evacuation routes, emergency contact lists, supply inventories, and real-time awareness of local hazards. Unlike existing ideas in the database that focus on productivity, health, finance, or social networking, this app is dedicated exclusively to emergency preparedness and personal safety.

## Target Audience
- Homeowners and renters
- Families with children or elderly members
- Outdoor enthusiasts
- Anyone living in disaster-prone areas (flood zones
- earthquake regions
- wildfire areas)
- Individuals seeking better personal safety planning

## Key Features
1. **Emergency Plan Builder**: Create personalized evacuation routes, emergency contact lists, and family communication plans with templates for different scenarios (fire, earthquake, flood, severe weather)
2. **Supply Tracker & Checklist**: Monitor essential emergency supplies (water, food, medications, first aid) with expiration date alerts and shopping list generation
3. **Real-Time Safety Alerts**: Receive location-based emergency notifications from official sources (weather warnings, evacuation orders, safety recalls)

## Monetization Strategy
- **Freemium Model**: Basic features free, premium subscription ($4.99/month or $39.99/year) for advanced planning tools and unlimited alerts
- **Emergency Kits Marketplace**: Affiliate commissions from recommended emergency supply retailers
- **Corporate/Community Licensing**: B2B sales to HOAs, apartment complexes, and community organizations

## Tech Stack Suggestions
- **Frontend**: React Native (cross-platform mobile app), Flutter alternative
- **Backend**: Node.js with Express or Python FastAPI
- **Database**: PostgreSQL for structured data, Redis for caching real-time alerts
- **Maps & Location**: Google Maps API or Mapbox for evacuation routes and location-based services
- **Notifications**: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) for push notifications
- **External APIs**: FEMA API, National Weather Service API, local emergency service integrations
