# LegalLens AI

## Overview
LegalLens AI is an intelligent legal document analysis platform that uses AI to help individuals and small businesses understand, review, and manage legal documents without expensive lawyer consultations. It breaks down complex legal jargon into plain language, highlights risky terms, and suggests improvements.

## Problem Solved
Individuals and small businesses struggle to understand, review, and manage legal documents (contracts, NDAs, leases, terms of service) without expensive lawyers. Most existing solutions are either too expensive or too generic. LegalLens AI bridges the gap between free generic templates and expensive lawyer consultations by providing affordable, AI-powered document analysis.

## Target Audience
- Freelancers who need to review client contracts
- Small business owners managing vendor agreements
- Renters reviewing lease agreements
- Job seekers analyzing employment contracts
- Anyone who regularly signs legal documents but cannot afford legal counsel

## Key Features
1. **Clause Explanation**: AI-powered breakdown of legal jargon into simple, understandable language with examples
2. **Risk Assessment**: Automated scanning of contracts for red flags (auto-renewal clauses, liability caps, termination penalties) with severity ratings
3. **Document Comparison**: Side-by-side comparison of multiple versions or competing offers to highlight differences and changes
4. **Template Library**: Access to vetted legal templates for common documents (NDAs, service agreements, lease addendums)
5. **Legal History Tracking**: Maintain a personal library of all reviewed documents with search and retrieval capabilities

## Monetization Strategy
- **Freemium Model**: Free basic clause explanation for short documents; premium features for advanced analysis
- **Subscription Tiers**: Monthly/annual subscriptions for unlimited document reviews, priority support, and template access
- **Pay-per-Document**: One-time fee for complex document analysis (e.g., $5-10 per contract)
- **B2B Licensing**: Enterprise plans for law firms or legal tech integrations
