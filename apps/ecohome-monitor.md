# EcoHome Monitor

## Overview
EcoHome Monitor is an AI-powered smart home energy management system that tracks, analyzes, and optimizes household energy consumption in real-time. It provides actionable insights to reduce utility bills while minimizing environmental impact through intelligent automation and predictive analytics.

## Problem Solved
- Rising electricity and utility costs for homeowners
- Lack of visibility into energy usage patterns
- Difficulty identifying wasteful energy consumption
- Limited tools for sustainable living optimization
- No integrated system for automated energy-saving measures

## Target Audience
- Environmentally conscious homeowners seeking to reduce carbon footprint
- Renters and property managers looking to optimize energy efficiency
- Tech-savvy users interested in smart home automation
- Families aiming to lower monthly utility bills
- Homeowners with solar panels wanting better energy management

## Key Features
1. **Real-Time Energy Monitoring**: Track electricity, water, and gas usage across all connected devices with instant visual feedback and alerts for abnormal consumption patterns.
2. **AI-Powered Optimization**: Machine learning algorithms analyze usage patterns to automatically adjust smart devices (thermostats, lights, appliances) for optimal energy efficiency without sacrificing comfort.
3. **Predictive Analytics & Insights**: Forecast future energy costs based on weather, usage trends, and utility rates; provide personalized recommendations for reducing consumption and saving money.
4. **Carbon Footprint Tracker**: Calculate household carbon emissions in real-time with visualizations showing environmental impact reduction over time and comparison to national averages.
5. **Smart Automation Rules**: Create custom automation scenarios (e.g., "turn off all lights when no motion detected," "pre-cool home before peak electricity hours") that execute automatically based on schedules, occupancy, or energy prices.

## Monetization Strategy
- **Freemium Model**: Basic monitoring features free; premium AI optimization and advanced analytics at $4.99/month
- **Hardware Sales**: Sell dedicated energy monitoring hub device ($79 one-time)
- **Affiliate Partnerships**: Earn commissions from recommended energy-efficient products and services
- **B2B Licensing**: Offer enterprise version for property management companies and HOAs
- **Utility Program Integration**: Partner with utilities offering rebates for energy-efficient homes

## Tech Stack Suggestions
- **Frontend**: React Native (mobile app), React.js (web dashboard)
- **Backend**: Node.js with Express, Python for AI/ML components
- **Database**: PostgreSQL (relational data), InfluxDB (time-series energy data)
- **IoT Integration**: MQTT protocol, Home Assistant API, Zigbee/Z-Wave support
- **AI/ML**: TensorFlow or PyTorch for predictive models and optimization algorithms
- **Cloud**: AWS IoT Core, Google Cloud Platform
- **Other**: WebSockets for real-time updates, Chart.js for visualizations
